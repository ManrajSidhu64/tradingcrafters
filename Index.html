03 / Technical analysis

Levels & price structure

Learn to map the areas that matter before making a chart decision.

Support

A price area where demand has previously appeared and declines have paused. Look for multiple reactions.

Resistance

A price area where supply has previously appeared and rallies have stalled. Breakouts can fail.

Breakout & retest

Price moves beyond a level, then may revisit it. A retest is not guaranteed to hold.

Moving average

A smoothed average of past prices. It helps show direction but lags and does not predict the future.
